# About XAICG

## What XAICG is

XAICG, in full X AI Comment/Reply Generator for Twitter, is a free Chrome extension. It adds an AI Reply button to every post on X. One click writes a draft reply in your own voice and types it into the reply box. From the toolbar it also writes posts and threads from a rough idea, and tightens drafts you wrote yourself.

It exists because replying is where most of the value of X is, and it is also the part that takes the time. Reading a post, finding something worth saying about it and saying it well is real work. A good first draft removes the blank box and leaves you the part that matters: deciding what you actually think.

## Why it is free, with no limit

Most AI tools send your text to a server, where every draft costs money, so they cap you or charge you. XAICG writes with an AI model that runs on your own computer. A draft costs nobody anything, so there is nothing to charge for and nothing to count down. There are no plans, no trial and no ads.

## What we hold to

**You post, not the tool.** XAICG never posts, likes or follows for you. A draft sits in the reply box until you press Reply, and a copy-only mode keeps it off the page altogether.

**It should sound like you.** A reply that reads like an assistant wrote it does more harm than no reply. XAICG writes from a persona you describe, reacts to one concrete detail in the post, and avoids the stock phrases that give AI writing away.

**Your writing stays yours.** The post you answer, your persona and your drafts stay on your computer. The only thing our server learns is how many installs there are and how many drafts they write, never what they say. The [privacy policy](/privacy) has the details.

**No setup beyond one click.** No account, no API key. You install it, press Set up once, and it works.
