# About XAICG

## What XAICG is

XAICG, in full X AI Comment/Reply Generator for Twitter, is a Chrome extension. It adds an AI Reply button to every post on X. One click writes a draft reply in your own voice and types it into the reply box. From the toolbar it also writes posts and threads from a rough idea, and tightens drafts you wrote yourself.

It exists because replying is where most of the value of X is, and it is also the part that takes the time. Reading a post, finding something worth saying about it and saying it well is real work. A good first draft removes the blank box and leaves you the part that matters: deciding what you actually think.

## What we hold to

**You post, not the tool.** XAICG never posts, likes or follows for you. A draft sits in the reply box until you press Reply, and a copy-only mode keeps it off the page altogether.

**It should sound like you.** A reply that reads like an assistant wrote it does more harm than no reply. XAICG writes from a persona you describe, reacts to one concrete detail in the post, and avoids the stock phrases that give AI writing away.

**No setup.** There is no API key to find and nothing to configure. You install it, sign in, and it works.

**Free to use every day.** Everyone gets {{free}} comments a day at no cost, with every feature. Paid plans exist for people who need more: {{ladder}}.

**Plain about data.** XAICG reads the one post you click and nothing else. The server does not store that post or the draft. There is no advertising and no sale of data. The [privacy policy](/privacy) says what is kept and why, in ordinary language.

## How XAICG pays for itself

Writing a draft costs money each time, because the text is produced by a large language model. The free plan is paid for by the people who choose a paid plan. That is the whole business: there are no ads in XAICG and your data is not the product.
