Features

An AI comment generator built for the way you use X

Everything happens where you already are: in the timeline, on the post you want to answer.

One click on any post

AI Reply and Question buttons sit in each post's action bar. The draft lands in X's own reply box, ready for you to read.

A tone for the moment

Auto fits each post. Or pick casual, witty, supportive, skeptical, analytical, blunt, technical or professional.

Try another, for free

Each request brings back several drafts. Step through them without using another comment from your allowance.

Posts, threads and rewrites

The toolbar popup turns a rough idea into post drafts or a full thread, and tightens a draft you already wrote.

Tones

Pick a tone when you want one

Most of the time Auto reads the room. When you want something specific, the arrow next to the buttons opens the tones, and the same menu copies a line for a quote post.

  • Eight tones, plus Auto
  • A short, specific question with one click
  • English by default, or the post's own language, or one of 17 you choose
Compose

Write the post you meant to write

Open XAICG from the toolbar and say what the post should be about. You get drafts to copy or open in X, a thread of the length you choose, or a tighter version of something you already wrote.

  • Posts in three lengths
  • Threads from three to eight posts
  • Rewrites that keep your facts, names and links
Your voice

Sounds like you, not like an assistant

XAICG is built to avoid the tells. No generic praise, no hype words, no em dashes, no hashtags you did not ask for. It reacts to one concrete detail in the post, the way a person does, and it remembers your recent drafts so it does not repeat itself.

Describe your persona once, or start from a preset and edit it. Our guide to making AI replies sound like you shows how.

Privacy

Nothing is posted for you

What XAICG sees, and what it keeps.

Only the post you click

XAICG reads the post you ask it to answer, and nothing else on the page. It never reads your messages, followers or account details, and it cannot post for you.

Drafts are not stored

The post's text goes to the XAICG server and on to OpenAI to write the draft. The server does not save that text or the draft it returns.

No API key of yours

You never paste an OpenAI key into the extension, so there is no key of yours to leak or to run up a bill.

Plain about the rest

What is kept, and why, is set out in the privacy policy in ordinary language.

FAQ

Questions

Is XAICG free?

Yes. Everyone gets {{free}} comments a day for nothing, with every tone and every writing mode, and no card is needed. If you need more than that, {{ladder}}.

Does XAICG post for me?

No. It types a draft into the reply box and stops. You decide whether to post it, edit it or throw it away. If you prefer, switch to copy-only mode and it will not touch the page at all.

Do I need an OpenAI API key?

No. The writing happens on the XAICG server. There is nothing to set up and no key to manage.

What counts as a comment?

Each time XAICG writes for you: a reply, a question, a post, a thread or a rewrite. One request brings back several drafts, and choosing "try another" to see the next one does not use another comment.

When do my comments come back?

Every allowance, free or paid, starts again at 00:00 UTC each day. Comments you did not use do not carry over.

Can I use my plan on more than one computer?

Yes, up to the number of browsers in your plan. Sign in with Google on each one. If all your seats are taken you can move the license to the browser you are on.

Which languages does it write in?

English by default. You can have it match the language of the post instead, or always write in one of 17 languages.

What happens to the posts I reply to?

The text of the post is sent to the XAICG server and to OpenAI so a draft can be written. It is not saved. The privacy policy has the details.

Is XAICG made by X?

No. XAICG is an independent product of Unique Labs and is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp.
