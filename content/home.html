Features

A free AI comment generator built for the way you use X

Everything happens where you already are: in the timeline, on the post you want to answer.

One click on any post

AI Reply and Question buttons sit in each post's action bar. The draft lands in X's own reply box, ready for you to read.

A tone for the moment

Auto fits each post. Or pick casual, witty, supportive, skeptical, analytical, blunt, technical or professional.

Try another, instantly

Each click brings back several drafts. Step through them in a moment, and ask for more as often as you like.

Posts, threads and rewrites

The toolbar popup turns a rough idea into post drafts or a full thread, and tightens a draft you already wrote.

Your voice, saved once

Describe how you sound. Set reply length, language and emoji. It applies to everything XAICG writes.

You stay in control

Nothing is ever posted for you. Every draft waits in the box until you press Reply. A copy-only mode never touches the page.

How it works

Up and running in a few minutes

  1. 1

    Add XAICG to Chrome

    Install the free extension from the Chrome Web Store. There is no account to make and no API key to find.

  2. 2

    Press Set up, once

    XAICG's AI model is downloaded to your computer. It takes a few minutes, one time, and then it works right there.

  3. 3

    Click AI Reply on any post

    A draft appears in the reply box. Keep it, edit it, or ask for another, as often as you like. You press Reply.

Your voice

Sounds like you, not like an assistant

XAICG is built to avoid the tells. No generic praise, no hype words, no em dashes, no hashtags you did not ask for. It reacts to one concrete detail in the post, the way a person does, and it remembers your recent drafts so it does not repeat itself.

Privacy

Nothing is posted for you

What XAICG sees, and what it keeps.

Only the post you click

XAICG reads the post you ask it to answer, and nothing else on the page. It never reads your messages, followers or account details, and it cannot post for you.

Written on your computer

The draft is written by XAICG's on-device AI, on your own computer. The post and the draft stay there.

Counts, not content

To know how many people use it, XAICG tells its server that an install exists and how many drafts of each kind it wrote. Never what they say, and nothing about you.

Plain about the rest

Everything XAICG keeps, and why, is in the privacy policy, in ordinary language.

FAQ

Questions

Is XAICG really free?

Yes, free forever, with no limit. There are no plans, no trial and no ads. The writing is done by an AI model on your own computer, so a draft costs nothing to make.

Does XAICG post for me?

No. It types a draft into the reply box and stops. You decide whether to post it, edit it or throw it away. If you prefer, switch to copy-only mode and it will not touch the page at all.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no key to paste. Install it, press Set up once, and start replying.

Where does the AI run?

On your computer. XAICG's AI model is downloaded once, the first time you press Set up, and from then on every draft is written right there. That is why it can be free, unlimited and private.

Will it work on my computer?

It needs desktop Chrome on Windows, macOS, Linux or a Chromebook Plus, about 22 GB of free disk space, and either a graphics card with more than 4 GB of memory or 16 GB of RAM. XAICG's settings tell you straight away if your computer can run it. Phones and tablets are not supported.

Which languages does it write in?

English, Spanish, German, French and Japanese. Choose one, or have it match the language of each post.

What happens to the posts I reply to?

Nothing leaves your computer: the post is read by the on-device AI right there, and the draft is written there. The privacy policy has the details.

Is XAICG made by X?

No. XAICG is an independent product of Unique Labs and is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp.
