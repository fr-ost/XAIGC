# Support

Most problems have a quick fix. Find yours below. If it is not there, write to us with the details listed at the bottom of this page.

## Getting started

### Where is XAICG after I install it?

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin XAICG. Then open x.com: every post has **AI Reply** and **Question** buttons. The XAICG icon in the toolbar opens the popup, where you write posts and threads. **Settings** is at the bottom of the popup.

### How do I start using it for free?

Click the XAICG icon and press **Sign in with Google**. Chrome asks which account to use. You get 5 free comments every day, with no card to enter. The free allowance is one per Google account.

### Sign in with Google does not work

- XAICG signs in through Chrome itself, so Chrome has to be signed in to a Google account. Use the profile button at the top right of Chrome.
- This kind of sign-in works in Google Chrome. Other browsers built on Chrome do not offer it.
- If you closed Google's prompt, press the button again.

## On X

### The AI Reply buttons are missing

- Reload the tab. The extension only joins pages opened after it was installed or updated.
- Check that XAICG is switched on at `chrome://extensions`.
- It works on x.com and twitter.com, in Chrome 111 or newer.
- X changes its pages from time to time. When that breaks the buttons we update the extension, and Chrome installs the update by itself.

### The draft was copied instead of typed into the reply box

That happens when XAICG cannot get to X's reply box. Paste the draft with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac). If you would rather always paste, open Settings and choose **Copy to the clipboard** under On x.com.

### The draft does not sound like me

Open Settings and describe your persona in your own words, or start from a preset and edit it. Set your default tone, reply length and language there too. For a different take on the same post, press the round button beside the reply box: it shows another draft and does not use another generation.

### It asks me to wait, or says it is busy

XAICG writes one draft at a time for each browser, with a short pause between them. Wait a second and click again.

### How do I turn the sounds off?

Open Settings and switch off **Sound effects** under On x.com.

## Plans and licenses

### I have used today's free comments, or reached today's limit

The free plan is 5 comments a day. Starter is 60 a day, Pro 200 and Agency 600. Every allowance starts again at 00:00 UTC, and the popup shows how many you have left. If 5 a day is not enough, the plans are listed at https://xaicg.uniquelabs.org/#pricing

### What counts as a comment?

Each time XAICG writes for you: a reply, a question, a post, a thread or a rewrite. One request brings back several drafts, and stepping through them with the round button beside the reply box does not use another one.

### I bought a license but I am still on the free plan

- Sign in with the Google account whose email address you gave when you bought. The license belongs to that account.
- Open Settings and press **Refresh**.
- Or open **I have a license key** in Settings, paste your key and press **Activate**.

### It says the license is in use on its maximum number of devices

Each plan covers a number of browsers. In Settings you can move the license to the browser you are on, and the browser that has been idle longest gives it up. To free a seat yourself, open Settings on a browser you no longer use and press **Remove license from this browser**.

### I lost my license key

If you sign in with Google you do not need the key: the license follows your account. Otherwise write to us from the email address you bought with and we will send you a new key. The old one stops working.

### Refunds and billing

Write to us from the email address you bought with. How refunds are handled is set out in the terms: https://xaicg.uniquelabs.org/terms

## When something is wrong

### "Can't reach the XAICG server"

Check your internet connection and try again in a minute. If other sites work and this keeps happening, tell us.

### "This version of XAICG is out of date"

Chrome updates extensions by itself, usually within a day. To update now, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, and press **Update**.

### Delete my account and data

Write to us from your account's email address, or send the Install ID shown in Settings under About. We erase your Google account ID, name and email address. The privacy policy has the details: https://xaicg.uniquelabs.org/privacy

## Writing to us

So that we can help in the first reply, please include:

- your Install ID and the extension's version, both shown in Settings under About
- what you clicked and what happened, with the exact message if there was one
- a screenshot, if you can
