# Support

Most problems have a quick fix. Find yours below. If it is not there, write to us with the details listed at the bottom of this page.

## Getting started

### Where is XAICG after I install it?

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin XAICG. Then open x.com: every post has **AI Reply** and **Question** buttons. The XAICG icon in the toolbar opens the popup, where you write posts and threads. **Settings** is at the bottom of the popup.

### How do I set it up?

After installing, the settings page opens. Under **One step left: set up the AI**, press **Set up**. The AI model is downloaded to your computer once, with a progress bar; it can take a few minutes. When the card says **Ready: free and unlimited**, you are done. There is no account to make.

### Is it really free? Is there a limit?

Yes, it is free, and there is no limit. There are no plans, no trial and no paid version. The AI runs on your own computer, so a draft costs nothing to make.

## Setting up the AI

### It says "Not available on this computer"

XAICG's AI runs on your computer, and this computer cannot run it. It needs:

- desktop Chrome on Windows 10 or 11, macOS 13 or later, Linux, or a Chromebook Plus
- about 22 GB of free disk space
- a graphics card with more than 4 GB of memory, or 16 GB of memory and 4 processor cores

Phones and tablets are not supported. If you have freed up space or changed computers, open Settings again: it checks each time.

### It says "Update Chrome to use XAICG"

Your version of Chrome is too old to run XAICG's AI. Open Chrome's menu, then **Help**, **About Google Chrome**. Chrome updates itself; restart it when asked, then open XAICG again.

### The download stopped, or seems stuck

- The download carries on in the background. Leave Chrome open for a few minutes, then press **Check again**.
- Make sure there is about 22 GB of free disk space.
- Check your internet connection and press **Set up** again.

### It worked before, and now asks to be set up again

The AI model can be removed when disk space runs very low. Free some space and press **Set up** again.

## On X

### The AI Reply buttons are missing

- Reload the tab. The extension only joins pages opened after it was installed or updated.
- Check that XAICG is switched on at `chrome://extensions`.
- It works on x.com and twitter.com, in desktop Chrome.
- X changes its pages from time to time. When that breaks the buttons we update the extension, and Chrome installs the update by itself.

### The buttons look dimmed

The AI is not set up yet. Hover a button to see why, or open XAICG's settings and press **Set up**.

### The draft was copied instead of typed into the reply box

That happens when XAICG cannot get to X's reply box. Paste the draft with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac). If you would rather always paste, open Settings and choose **Copy to the clipboard** under On x.com.

### The draft does not sound like me

Open Settings and describe your persona in your own words, or start from a preset and edit it. Set your default tone, reply length and language there too. For a different take on the same post, press the round button beside the reply box: it shows another draft straight away.

### It says the post is in a language XAICG doesn't write

XAICG writes English, Spanish, German, French and Japanese. If your language setting is **Match the post's language** and the post is in another language, choose one of those five in Settings instead.

### It is slow

The AI runs on your computer, so a computer without a fast graphics card takes longer, sometimes several seconds. XAICG writes one draft at a time; wait for the first before asking for the next.

### How do I turn the sounds off?

Open Settings and switch off **Sound effects** under On x.com.

## Privacy

### Does XAICG send my posts anywhere?

No. The post you answer, your settings and your drafts stay on your computer. The XAICG server is only told that your install exists and how many drafts of each kind it wrote, never what they say. The privacy policy has the details: https://xaicg.uniquelabs.org/privacy

### How do I delete my data?

Uninstall XAICG. That removes everything it stored on your computer. There is no account, and nothing on the server can be linked to you.
