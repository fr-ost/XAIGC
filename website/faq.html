Frequently asked questions

Short answers about what XAICG does, why it costs nothing and what happens to your writing. For step-by-step fixes, see support.

The basics

What is XAICG?

XAICG is a free Chrome extension that writes draft replies, comments, posts and threads for X (Twitter). It adds an AI Reply button and a Question button to every post. You click one, a draft is typed into X's own reply box, and you decide what happens next. Its full name is X AI Comment/Reply Generator for Twitter.

How do I start?

Add XAICG to Chrome. The settings page opens: press Set up once, and the AI model is downloaded to your computer. Then open x.com and press AI Reply on any post. There is no account to make and no API key to find.

Does XAICG post for me?

No. It types a draft into the reply box and stops. You read it, change it if you want, and press Reply yourself. If you would rather it never touched the page, switch to copy-only mode in Settings and paste the draft where you want it.

Can it write posts and threads, or only replies?

All three. Click the XAICG icon in Chrome's toolbar, type a rough idea, and it writes post drafts in three lengths or a thread of three to eight posts. It can also rewrite a draft of your own so it reads better and keeps your facts, names and links.

Free and unlimited

Is XAICG really free?

Yes, free forever. There are no plans, no trial, no credits and no ads. Every feature is included.

Is there a limit?

No. Reply to as many posts as you like, every day, and press "try another" as often as you want.

How can it be free?

Most AI tools send your text to a server, and every draft costs their maker money, so they limit you or charge you. XAICG's AI runs on your own computer. A draft costs nobody anything, so there is nothing to charge for. More on this in what on-device AI is.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no key to paste.

Your computer

Will it work on my computer?

It needs desktop Chrome on Windows 10 or 11, macOS 13 or later, Linux or a Chromebook Plus, about 22 GB of free disk space, and either a graphics card with more than 4 GB of memory or 16 GB of memory and 4 processor cores. XAICG's settings page tells you straight away whether your computer can run it.

Does it work on my phone?

No. Chrome on Android and iPhone cannot run XAICG's AI. It works in desktop Chrome.

Why does setup download something?

The AI model has to be on your computer to write there. It is downloaded once, when you press Set up, and then works without sending anything anywhere.

Is it slow?

On a computer with a good graphics card, a draft takes a moment. Without one it can take several seconds. XAICG writes one draft at a time.

Writing quality

Will the replies sound like me?

That is the point of it. In Settings you describe your persona in your own words, or start from a preset and edit it, and you set your usual tone, reply length, language and emoji. XAICG is built to avoid the usual signs of AI writing: no generic praise, no hype words, no hashtags you did not ask for. It reacts to one concrete detail in the post, the way a person does. See how to make AI replies sound like you.

What tones are there?

Auto picks a tone to fit each post. You can also choose casual, witty, supportive, skeptical, analytical, blunt, technical or professional from the arrow next to the buttons.

Which languages does it write in?

English, Spanish, German, French and Japanese. Choose one in Settings, or have it match the language of each post.

What if I do not like a draft?

Press "try another". Each click brings back several drafts, and you can ask again as often as you like. You can also pick a different tone, or simply edit the text: it is in your reply box, and it is yours.

Does it repeat itself?

It tries not to. Your recent drafts are kept on your computer and taken into account, so the new draft does not repeat their openings and phrasing. You can clear that memory in Settings at any time.

Privacy and safety

What does XAICG read on X?

Only the post you click, so that it can answer it. It does not read your direct messages, your followers, your password or the rest of the page.

Is the post I reply to sent anywhere?

No. The post, your persona and your drafts stay on your computer, where the draft is written. The XAICG server is only told that your install exists and how many drafts of each kind it wrote, never what they say. The privacy policy has the details.

Is it safe for my X account?

XAICG never posts, likes, follows or sends anything by itself. Every reply is one you read and sent by hand. X has its own rules about spam and automation, and you remain responsible for what you post, so do not use any tool to send the same message to many people. We wrote about this in is it safe to use AI for replies on X.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall XAICG: that removes everything it stored on your computer. There is no account, and nothing on our server can be linked to you.

Is XAICG made by X?

No. XAICG is an independent product of Unique Labs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by X Corp.
