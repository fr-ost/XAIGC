Help and support

XAICG is free, with no account and no limit. Drafts are written on this computer by XAICG's on-device AI: the post you answer, your settings and your drafts stay here. The XAICG server is only told that this install exists and how many drafts of each kind it wrote, never what they say. XAICG never posts for you and does not read your messages or account data.
